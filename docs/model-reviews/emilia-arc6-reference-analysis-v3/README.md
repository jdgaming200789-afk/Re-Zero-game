# Emilia V3: S1 neutral evidence added, model unchanged

**All guides remain unapproved. No model edits until explicit guide approval.**

The supplied S4/Arc 6 anime art remains authoritative. The two newly supplied
S1 production sheets provide secondary structural evidence. The neutral side
is useful because it removes the trunk lean seen in the S4 running and leaning
frames. It does not expose a nude torso: the older fitted bodice covers the
front, while hair and cape obstruct the rear. If S1 visibly disagrees with S4,
follow S4. Neither the S1 cup outline nor its fitted bodice is an S4 body target.

`emilia-revised-guides-v3.png` shows FRONT, 3/4 and SIDE hypotheses. The S1
neutral side is directly beside the SIDE proposal. A support matrix separates
S4 primary evidence, S1 secondary evidence and hidden interpolation for the
upper onset, projection region, lower return and total torso depth. Blue traces
on the S1 reference are costume edges; they are not magenta body targets.
The V2 paths are retained unchanged. The added evidence does not justify a new
numerical depth fit to the S1 bodice. Magenta paths and amber uncertainty bands
remain proposals, not measured anatomy or approved mesh coordinates.

`emilia-neutral-side-evidence-v3.png` gives explicitly defined dimensionless
image checks. For S1, H is the visible upper lateral bodice rim to waist-trim
span, and u is downward image distance divided by H. Covered chest width Wc,
fitted bodice width Wb and forward costume excursion E are manually marked.
E is measured from the visible lower-front bodice line, not a guessed spine
or torso centreline. It cannot measure total anatomical depth.

These ratios compare visible art, not anatomical chest or waist measurements.
Different S1 and S4 costume boundaries must not be averaged or transferred.
The two S1 sheets repeat the same design, so they are not independent body
scans. Manual source landmarks have about +/- 2 source pixels of reading
ambiguity; that is not a statistical confidence interval. Low resolution,
stroke width, occlusion and garment structure prevent precise body fitting.
No official physical measurements are claimed, and no fan measurements were
used. `analysis-v3.json` records the definitions, original pixel landmarks,
computed ratios, traces, guide coordinates and source-image SHA-256 hashes.

The supported constraint is the sequence of covered upper fullness within an
upright torso, checked against the S4 exposed purple segments and oblique
views. Gentle hidden-body onset, exact medial/outer depth and the long smooth
lower return remain interpolation. The sharp S1 cup edge does not justify an
anatomical notch or horizontal shelf. Front, side and oblique proposals must
eventually agree as one surface; no 2D costume ratio can settle that alone.

mesh informed this analysis. No Blender build or render was run. SHA-256
checks before and after confirm all 30 tracked character model sources and
assets are identical to the starting working tree at
`b6b071278bfa1bf10c784c27b7e8cdfbfcac226d`. Waist, abdomen, pelvis, hips,
