# Vertical Slice — "The Watchtower in the Sand"

A 25–35 minute, self-contained chapter covering the party's arrival at the Pleiades
Watchtower, built to exercise every core system once, at full quality.

## Canon anchors

- The tower stands at the eastern end of the **Augria Sand Dunes**, a region of miasma,
  witchbeasts and the time-distorting **Sand Time**. Floors are numbered from the top:
  **Maia** (1, top, Volcanica), **Electra** (2, Reid Astrea's trial), **Taygeta** (3,
  the monolith trial, then the Taygeta Library of the Books of the Dead), **Alcyone**
  (4, living quarters, dining hall, the healing **Green Room**), **Celaeno** (5, the
  ground floor), **Asterope** (6, basement).
- The guardian **Shaula** greets Subaru as "Master" and enforces the tower's rules;
  breaking them turns her into Scorpio. She has watched the sands for four hundred
  years, and her **Heliosphere** — a white light from the tower — is what the party
  first sees of her.
- The **Sand Earthworm** is a 20–30 m witchbeast that lurks beneath the sand.
- Taygeta's trial: *"Touch upon the greatest splendour of the hero destroyed by
  Shaula."* The room fills with star-pillars; the answer is **Rigel**, Orion's
  brightest star (Orion, slain by the scorpion). Only someone from Subaru's world
  could know it.
- Party: Subaru, Emilia, Beatrice, Julius (whose name the world has forgotten), Ram,
  Anastasia (with Echidna the artificial spirit speaking through her), Meili,
  Patrasche; Rem, comatose, travels with them.

The slice compresses and adapts events for play; it does not contradict the arc's
premise, relationships or rules.

## Flow

1. **Tower's Foot — camp (night).** Cinematic conversation at the dragon carriage:
   the tower finally visible after the Sand Time; motives (Rem, Julius's name, the
   Sage). Quest *The Watchtower in the Sand* begins. **Return point / checkpoint.**
2. **Exploration — the outer ruins.** Half-buried terraces, fallen colossi, old
   campsites of those who never arrived; lore objects and clues; party chatter.
   Inventory interaction: the **Carriage Bell** (and consumables).
3. **The Glass Flats (stealth/survival).** Open sand fused into glass by centuries of
   white light. Loop 1: crossing in the open draws a star-glint from the summit and
   the Heliosphere kills Subaru → **Return by Death** sequence → back at camp.
   Knowledge gained (`know.heliosphere.*`): the light tracks *movement in the open*;
   the summit glints ~1.5 s before it fires; ruins that block the summit's view are
   safe. Loop 2: a new dialogue option to warn the party; the detection telegraph is
   now readable; cross cover-to-cover.
4. **Gate plaza — combat encounter.** A witchbeast pack (dune jackals) attacks;
   party AI fights with distinct identities; Subaru commands and supports.
5. **Elite: the Sand Earthworm.** Too tough to out-damage. It hunts by vibration
   through sand. The answer uses loop-1 knowledge: lure it (bell / noise) onto the
   Glass Flats where the Heliosphere strikes whatever moves in the open.
6. **Celaeno.** Shaula's arrival ("Master!") — cinematic. The tower's rules.
   **Checkpoint.**
7. **Alcyone.** Carry Rem to the Green Room — party interaction (Emilia, Beatrice).
   Atmospheric transition from cold night to warm, lived-in quarters. Balcony view
   of the dunes.
8. **Taygeta.** White room, monolith, the star-pillar puzzle (observation +
   information; party members speculate; Subaru's Earth knowledge). Completion
   transforms the void into the **Taygeta Library** — end-of-slice hook.
